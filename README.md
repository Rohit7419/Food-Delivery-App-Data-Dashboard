# Food-Delivery-App-Data-Dashboard
# 🍔 Food Delivery Data Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

### 🍔 Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze food delivery business data, providing insights into **orders, revenue, customers, restaurant performance, delivery operations, and business trends**.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard is an interactive **Power BI report** developed to transform food delivery data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard enables users to explore sales performance, customer behavior, restaurant performance, and delivery operations through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform used for dashboard development and data visualization.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
* 🧠 **DAX** – Used to create calculated measures, KPIs, and analytical metrics.
* 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Used to structure and connect data for analysis and interactive filtering.
* 📈 **Data Visualization** – Used charts, KPI cards, slicers, maps, and interactive visuals to communicate insights.
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for Power BI development and `.png` for dashboard previews.

---

## 4. Data Source

The project uses a **Food Delivery App dataset** containing transactional and operational information related to food orders and customers.

The dataset includes information related to:

* Orders and order volume
* Revenue and sales performance
* Customers
* Restaurants
* Locations / cities
* Delivery performance
* Order and customer trends
* Restaurant performance

The dataset was analyzed and transformed to create an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Food delivery businesses generate large amounts of data related to orders, customers, restaurants, revenue, and delivery operations. Analyzing this information through raw data can make it difficult to quickly identify **sales trends, customer behavior, top-performing restaurants, and operational issues**.

### • Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of the dashboard is to provide an interactive analytical tool that helps users:

* Monitor overall food delivery business performance.
* Analyze revenue and order trends.
* Understand customer behavior and distribution.
* Identify top-performing restaurants.
* Analyze delivery and operational performance.
* Compare performance across different locations.
* Support data-driven business decisions.

### • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

* **Executive Overview**
  Provides a high-level summary of important business KPIs such as orders, revenue, customers, and overall performance.

* **Customer Analytics**
  Analyzes customer behavior and trends to understand customer distribution and ordering patterns.

* **Restaurant Performance**
  Identifies high-performing restaurants and compares restaurant-level performance using relevant business metrics.

* **Delivery & Operations**
  Provides insights into delivery performance and operational metrics to help evaluate the efficiency of the food delivery process.

* **Location / City Analysis**
  Enables comparison of business performance across different cities and locations.

* **Trend Analysis**
  Interactive charts help identify changes in orders, revenue, and other business metrics over time.

* **Interactive Filters & Slicers**
  Users can filter the dashboard by different dimensions to explore specific segments of the food delivery business.

### • Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

* Overall order and revenue performance.
* Trends in food delivery orders over time.
* Top-performing restaurants and locations.
* Customer ordering patterns.
* Delivery and operational performance.
* Areas with opportunities for business improvement.

### • Business Impact

**Performance Monitoring:** Business managers can quickly monitor important KPIs and track overall performance.

**Customer Insights:** Customer analysis can help businesses understand ordering behavior and identify important customer segments.

**Restaurant Optimization:** Restaurant-level analysis helps identify top performers and areas requiring improvement.

**Operational Improvement:** Delivery analysis can highlight operational trends and potential delivery-efficiency issues.

**Data-Driven Decision Making:** The dashboard converts raw food delivery data into interactive insights that can support strategic business decisions.
